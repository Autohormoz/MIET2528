Joining a group

Groups are student-led — you coordinate directly with teammates, not through the lecturer, and groups form from students in your own workshop timeslot (groups of 5). Joining in Week 3 is completely normal and expected. The practical deadline is end of Week 4, since the Week 6 Progress Report needs a group in place well before then. Register your group here — can't find a team? Talk to your lab assistants closer to the deadline.
