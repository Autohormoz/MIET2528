RMIT Peer Mentoring

Free support from students who've done your course before — help with assignments, study tips, or just someone who gets what you're going through right now.

Chat with a peer mentor who's been in your shoes

Need help with assignments, study tips, or just want to connect with someone who understands the course? You can now chat with a peer mentor who's already been through it. It's a great way to stay on top of things and make a new friend along the way.

Free and flexible Chat online or meet in person Friendly, experienced student mentors

How to connect — super easy

  1. Go to rmit.vygo.app
  2. Log in with your RMIT ID and password
  3. Go to the Programs tab
  4. Select the Peer Mentoring Program for your degree
  5. Click Join Program
  6. Say hi to your matched mentor, or browse mentor profiles!

Want to know more?

Full details are on the Peer Mentoring webpage. Having trouble? Email the team directly at peer.mentoring@rmit.edu.au, or follow @rmitpeermentoring on Instagram for tips from mentors and real mentoring stories.
