Format

  • 10% of your final grade, 60 minutes
  • Open book — hard copy notes only, no digital tools except a calculator
  • You'll get a hard copy to write your answers on — bring a pen or pencil and a calculator

What to study

Straight from the quiz's own description: force and moment systems, force analysis (FBD, support reactions, concentrated & distributed loading), and frames and machines.

  • Week 1 — Introduction to the Course and Force and Moment Systems
  • Week 2 — Force Analysis: Support Reactions (FBDs, concentrated loading)
  • Week 3 — Force Analysis: Internal Loading (distributed loading, joints and sections — the frames and machines material)
  • T01 and T02 on the Tutorials Hub — practice problems and worked solutions
