Your 100 minutes: 60 for the quiz, 30 extra time (your ELP), and 10 at the end to upload your answers. You can click "Begin Test" at any point in the window above — there's no advantage to starting early or late.

How to submit

  1. Write your answers on paper, as you normally would.
  2. Photograph or scan each answer.
  3. Upload the file under each question on Canvas, within your allowed time.

Lighting and readability of your photos is your own responsibility — check they're clear and in focus before submitting.

Good to know

  • One attempt only — take a moment to be ready before you click "Begin Test." The timer starts immediately and can't be paused.
  • Have pen, paper, and a calculator ready before you start.

Late submissions

Submissions are penalised 10% of the total mark for each minute of delay. Anything more than 10 minutes late can't be accepted — which is exactly why the last 10 minutes of your 100 are set aside for uploading.

How your answers are marked

  • Calculation questions are marked on the appropriateness of your approach and your final answers. Marks are awarded for working, so show it in full — marks are deducted for missing or incorrect units, missing or incorrect steps, and unclear or unlabelled sketches.
  • Written-response questions are marked on clarity, accuracy, technical detail, the quality of any figures, and how fully the question is answered. Grammar and English aren't marked down, except where they cause genuine confusion about the technical content.

What to study

Straight from the quiz's own description: force and moment systems, force analysis (FBD, support reactions, concentrated & distributed loading), and frames and machines.

  • Week 1 — Introduction to the Course and Force and Moment Systems
  • Week 2 — Force Analysis: Support Reactions
  • Week 3 — Force Analysis: Internal Loading
  • T01 and T02 on the Tutorials Hub
