Final Class Test — Week 12

Weight: 35% of your final mark

When: during Week 12 (12–18 October 2026), at your own timetabled lectorial session. You must sit it in your own session — tests aren't held online.

Duration: 10 minutes reading + 90 minutes writing

  • You'll get a hard copy of the test to write your answers on — bring a pen or pencil
  • Open book, open notes — hard copy only (handwritten or printed); no devices
  • Laptops, tablets and phones must be switched off and in your bag — having one on you, even in a pocket, breaches test conditions and can be reported as misconduct
  • Check the calculator policy to know exactly what's permitted

How it's marked

  • Calculation questions: marked on your solution approach and final answer. Full working must be shown for full marks — marks are deducted for missing/incorrect units, missing or incorrect steps, and unclear or unlabelled sketches
  • Written-response questions: marked on clarity, accuracy, technical detail, and how completely you've answered — not on grammar or English, unless it obscures the technical content
